Highlights for Aerospace Science and Technology (AST):

- Physics-hybrid flight control combines TECS, pitch-rate MRAC, and CNN-BiLSTM.
- Analytical Small-Gain stability bounds derived for elevator effectiveness loss.
- 6-DOF F-16 hold-trim verification contracts eliminate uncommanded climb drift.
- 20-seed Monte Carlo campaign under Dryden gusts achieves 6.3% ISE_h reduction.
- Open-source JSBSim simulation code, weights, and telemetry released on Zenodo.
