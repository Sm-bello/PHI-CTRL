---------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT THIS IS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Layered hybrid architecture for resilient longitudinal control under
actuator-effectiveness faults on JSBSim F-16A (6-DOF):

  L0 Plant       plant/jsbsim_plant_f16.py
  L1 Baseline    controller/energy_hold_f16.py
  L2 Resilient   MRAC + MMAE bank + optional PPO residual (MMAE-gated)
  L3 Integration phi_ctrl_unified_f16.py  +  optional FlightGear

Honest scope:
  - Detector = GainRatio + MMAE-style bank (NOT CNN-BiLSTM yet)
  - Observer = diagnostic-only (OBSERVER_CLOSED_LOOP=False)
  - Residual is MMAE-gated; full-stack is not assumed superior to baseline
  - CNN-BiLSTM twin and multi-aircraft campaigns are future work
